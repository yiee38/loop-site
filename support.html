Common questions

Where do I start?

Tap a pad: every pad on rack A plays a drum from the first launch. To see how a whole beat is put together, open the menu (≡ on the display) and choose How it works. It opens a new project built from the starter sounds, and your own projects stay as they were.

How do I record a sound?

Tap SOUND, then hold a pad. Noodle! records into that pad until you lift your finger, and keeps the whole take in your Library. To record without a pad, open Sounds, tap + and choose Record.

Noodle! doesn't pick anything up when I record.

Noodle! needs microphone access. Go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone and turn Noodle! on.

Can I use my own sounds?

Yes. Open Sounds, tap + and choose Import to bring in an audio file from Files. It lands in your Library, ready to put on a pad or cut into slices.

How do I send a recording to someone?

Open the menu (≡ on the display), choose Share recording and pick the recording. Noodle! makes an .m4a file, which plays on most phones and computers, and opens the share sheet so you can pick where it goes.

I play left-handed.

Open the menu and set Playing hand to Left. The keys and PLAY move under your left hand wherever one hand plays the pads.

I deleted a starter sound. Can I get it back?

Starter sounds are ordinary recordings, so a deleted one stays deleted. Deleting and reinstalling Noodle! adds them again, but it also erases everything else you've made.

How do I move my work to a new phone?

Noodle! keeps everything on your device and doesn't sync between devices. Your work moves with a backup: set up the new phone from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one.

The music stopped when a call came in.

A call, an alarm or another app taking the sound stops playback, so Noodle! never plays over it. Press PLAY to start again.

Does Noodle! collect my data?

No. Your work never leaves your device unless you share it. The Privacy Policy has the whole of it.
