Common questions
Noodle! doesn't pick up anything when I record.

Noodle! needs microphone access. Go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone and turn Noodle! on.

How do I send a recording to someone?

In the Library, touch and hold a recording and choose Share…. Noodle! makes an .m4a file, which plays on most phones and computers, and opens the share sheet so you can pick where it goes.

I deleted a starter sound. Can I get it back?

Starter sounds are ordinary sounds, so a deleted one stays deleted. Deleting and reinstalling Noodle! adds them again, but it also erases everything else you've made.

How do I move my work to a new phone?

Noodle! keeps everything on your device and doesn't sync between devices. Your work moves with a backup: set up the new phone from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one.

The music stopped when a call came in.

A phone call stops playback so Noodle! doesn't play over it. Press play to start again.

Does Noodle! collect my data?

Your work never leaves your device unless you share it. See the Privacy Policy for the whole of it.
