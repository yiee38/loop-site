The detail · effective 13 September 2026
What Noodle! keeps on your device

Your projects, patterns, scenes and the sounds in your Library are saved in Noodle!'s own storage on your iPhone or iPad. Noodle! never sends them over the internet, and we have no way to see them. Noodle! also saves one small setting that remembers the starter sounds have already been added, so a starter you delete stays deleted.

The microphone

Noodle! asks for microphone access so you can record sounds. It reads the microphone only while you are recording, and what it records is saved as an audio file on your device. Nothing is streamed, uploaded or analysed. You can turn access off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone.

Sharing a recording

Nothing you make leaves Noodle! unless you send it. When you touch and hold a recording in the Library and choose Share…, Noodle! makes an audio file (.m4a) and hands it to the iOS share sheet, where you pick where it goes. Noodle! does not choose the destination and is not told what you picked. The file carries the recording's name, the date it was recorded, and “Noodle!” as the app that made it. Once shared, it is handled by whichever app or person you sent it to.

How we improve Noodle!

Noodle! has no analytics or tracking code of its own. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers on your device, Apple gives us anonymous, aggregated statistics about how Noodle! is used and about any crashes. We use them only to find problems and make the app better. They do not identify you, and you can stop sharing them at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

If you test Noodle! through TestFlight, the feedback and crash reports you choose to send also reach us. If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply and to improve the app.

Backups and deleting your data

Like any app's data, what you make in Noodle! is included in your device's backups (iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer), which Apple handles under your own settings. To delete everything Noodle! has stored, delete the app.
