The detail

Effective 12 November 2026

What Noodle! keeps on your device

Your projects, with their patterns, chain and mix, and every recording in your Library are saved in Noodle!'s own storage on your iPhone or iPad. Noodle! never sends them over the internet, and we have no way to see them.

Noodle! also keeps a few small settings beside them: the hand you play with, which project was open, whether you have cleared a lane yet, so that hint stops showing, and whether the four starter recordings have been added, so a starter you delete stays deleted.

The microphone

Noodle! asks for microphone access so you can record sounds into a pad or into your Library. It uses the microphone only while a recording is being made, and what it records is saved as an audio file on your device. Nothing is streamed, uploaded or analysed. You can turn access off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone.

Sounds you import

When you choose Import on the Sounds page, iOS shows its own file picker, and Noodle! reads only the audio file you pick. It copies that file into your Library on your device and sees nothing else in your files.

Sharing a recording

Nothing you make leaves Noodle! unless you send it. When you choose Share recording in the menu and pick a recording, Noodle! makes an audio file (.m4a) and hands it to the iOS share sheet, where you pick where it goes. Noodle! does not choose the destination and is not told what you picked.

The file carries the recording's name, the date it was made, and “Noodle!” as the app that made it. After that, it is in the hands of the app or person you sent it to.

How we improve Noodle!

Noodle! has no analytics or tracking code of its own, and it does not connect to the internet. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers on your device, Apple gives us anonymous, aggregated statistics about how Noodle! is used and about any crashes. We use them only to find problems and make the app better. They do not identify you, and you can stop sharing them at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

If you test Noodle! through TestFlight, the feedback and crash reports you choose to send also reach us. If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply and to improve the app.

Backups and deleting your data

Like any app's data, what you make in Noodle! is included in your device's backups (iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer), which Apple handles under your own settings. To delete everything Noodle! has stored, delete the app.

Changes to this policy
